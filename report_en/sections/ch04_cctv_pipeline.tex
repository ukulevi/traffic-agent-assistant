\chapter{Tier 1: CCTV Pipeline and Tensor Builder}
\label{chapter:tier1}

\section{Edge Ingestion and Privacy Governance}
Tier 1 serves as the foundational data ingestion layer for STWI. It ingests traffic observation streams from up to 20 physical intersection cameras and aggregates environmental sensor feeds across the metropolitan network.

\subsection{Zero Raw-Video Retention Policy}
To comply with municipal privacy regulations and data protection mandates, STWI enforces an absolute \textbf{Zero Raw-Video Retention} policy:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5cm, label=$\bullet$]
  \item \textbf{Volatile In-Memory Processing:} Video streams (RTSP / pre-recorded MP4) are decoded frame-by-frame directly in volatile RAM buffers. No raw video frames, compressed video clips, or unblurred facial/license-plate crops are written to persistent disk storage.
  \item \textbf{Immediate Feature Aggregation:} Object detection (YOLOv8) and multi-object tracking (ByteTrack) algorithms extract vehicle bounding boxes, classifications (cars, motorcycles, buses, trucks), and instantaneous optical flow velocities.
  \item \textbf{Edge Extraction \& Purge:} Once object counts and harmonic average velocities are aggregated into 5-minute time bins, all underlying frame tensors are immediately deallocated from GPU memory.
\end{itemize}

\section{Tensor Builder and Spatial Alignment}
The extracted 5-minute scalar values are transformed into structured multi-dimensional arrays by the \textbf{Tensor Builder} module.

\subsection{Missing Data Handling and Mask Generation}
Urban sensor networks frequently experience transient packet loss, power outages, or camera occlusions. Rather than blindly zero-filling missing entries (which distorts neural network gradients), the Tensor Builder generates a companion boolean missing mask:
\begin{equation}
  M_{b,t,n,f} = 
  \begin{cases}
    1 & \text{if feature } f \text{ at node } n \text{ and timestep } t \text{ is observed}, \\
    0 & \text{if feature } f \text{ is missing / corrupted}.
  \end{cases}
\end{equation}
For downstream neural layers, missing values are imputed via spatial-temporal neighborhood interpolation (mean historical pattern combined with adjacent node flow ratios) while explicitly passing the mask tensor $M$ to allow attention mechanisms to down-weight imputed indices.

\subsection{Temporal Alignment and Sliding Window}
The pipeline maintains a rolling 60-minute historical buffer (12 consecutive 5-minute bins) for all 20 canonical intersections. Every 5 minutes, a new timestep slice is appended, the oldest slice is evicted, and the synchronized tensors $(X, M, A)$ are committed to TimescaleDB for continuous availability.
