\section{Evaluation Methodology and Metrics}
To rigorously validate STWI, the system is evaluated across three primary engineering dimensions: (1) predictive accuracy, (2) operational latency SLAs, and (3) safety verification robustness.

\subsection{Predictive Accuracy Metrics}
Predictive performance is evaluated via Coefficient of Determination ($R^2$), Mean Absolute Error (MAE), and Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE):
\begin{align}
  R^2 &= 1 - \frac{\sum_i (y_i - \hat{y}_i)^2}{\sum_i (y_i - \bar{y})^2} \\
  \text{MAE} &= \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N |y_i - \hat{y}_i| \\
  \text{RMSE} &= \sqrt{\frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N (y_i - \hat{y}_i)^2}
\end{align}

Across the 20-node test set, the GCN--LSTM baseline forecaster achieves an $R^2$ of **0.874** for traffic volume and **0.862** for velocity, demonstrating strong spatial-temporal fidelity.
