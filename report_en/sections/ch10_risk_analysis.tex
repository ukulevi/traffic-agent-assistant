\section{Risk Identification and Governance Strategy}
Deploying AI-driven decision-support systems into municipal transportation networks involves technical, legal, privacy, and operational risks. STWI maintains a proactive risk governance framework centered around the principle of **Fail-Closed Safety**.

\section{The 14-Item Comprehensive Risk Register}
Table \ref{tab:risk_register} details the 14 identified project risks, severity assessments, and concrete mitigation measures implemented:
